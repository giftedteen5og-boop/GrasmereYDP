Who We Are

GRASMERE is a community of approximately 300,000 people located in regions 07, 06, and 05 of the Johannesburg district, South Africa. Like many communities, we have talented young people, hardworking families, and a strong sense of unity. But we also face serious challenges and for too long, our area has been left out of development programs.

We started this initiative because we believe our community deserves the same opportunities as others. This website is our platform to connect with organizations that can help us grow and to document the work of every partner who comes to stand with us.

The Challenges We Face

Our community currently has no active NGO or youth development program operating in the area. This means:

  • Young people lack skills training, mentorship, and opportunities
  • Basic services and infrastructure are limited
  • Families struggle without support systems that other communities receive

We are not asking for pity we are asking for partnership. With the right programs, our community can thrive.

Why Work With Our Community?

  • We are organized and ready — we have a dedicated team coordinating with partners
  • We document everything — your work will be publicly showcased on this site
  • We are transparent — we share progress openly with partners and the community
  • We are committed for the long term — this is not a one-time request; we want lasting relationships
  • Our community is eager — residents are ready to participate and support programs
